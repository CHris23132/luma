Privacy is part of the design

No video recordings. No saved images.

Luma is designed to analyze the live camera view on the device, without recording or saving video or images. It looks for specific critical events that may need your attention.

  • Live activity is analyzed locally on the device
  • When a defined alert is detected, a short event log—not footage—is sent to the alert service
  • The alert service uses that log to notify your chosen contacts by text or call

Good questions

Before you bring it home.

A product for families should answer the practical questions plainly.

Does Luma replace watching my child?

No. It is intended as an assistive awareness tool for use alongside responsible supervision. It cannot detect every risk, prevent harm, or guarantee that an alert will arrive.

Will I need to watch a live feed?

The planned everyday experience is based on text and call alerts rather than an always-open dashboard. Final product features should be confirmed before launch.

How are alerts sent?

The planned flow is device-based detection, an alert event sent to a cloud service, and SMS or phone-call delivery to configured contacts. Connectivity and phone-service availability affect delivery.

Where do I add my phone number?

After purchase, the intended setup flow lets you pair your device and add a primary number, with an optional backup contact. Number verification should occur before alerts are enabled.

Does it call emergency services?

No emergency-services integration is represented here. If you think someone is in danger, contact local emergency services directly.
